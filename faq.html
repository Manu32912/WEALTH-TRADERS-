Frequently Asked Questions

Tap each question to read its answer.

How do I create an account?
Enter your name, email, international mobile number with country code, and a password. The browser sends the credentials to the secure backend over HTTPS; the server stores only a password hash.
How do I select an investment plan?
Open Investment, choose a plan, and you will be taken to the payment information page showing the selected plan. Review all terms and risks before transferring funds.
How do I join VIP?
Choose Monthly, 3 Months, or Lifetime. The Join VIP button takes you to the same payment information page used for investment plans.
Which crypto networks are supported?
The payment page identifies TRC20 and BEP20 as supported networks. Always verify the exact payment address and network before sending.
When will VIP show as paid?
VIP should show as paid only after the payment has been independently verified by the platform administrator. The website should never mark a payment as complete just because a user clicked a button.
Are investment targets guaranteed?
No. Any stated target or historical performance figure is not a guarantee of future returns. Trading and investment involve risk.
